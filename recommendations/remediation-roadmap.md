# Remediation Roadmap

## First 30 days

- Confirm every group entity, digital service, controller role, DPO channel, and notice owner.
- Build an inventory of public and contextual notices.
- Validate whether the statement on automated decision-making remains accurate.
- Confirm the legal source and business owner for every published retention period.

## Days 31 to 60

- Create a purpose-level processing matrix from the record of processing activities.
- Separate Article 6 lawful bases from Article 9 conditions.
- Define the audience modules and Layer 1 summary.
- Reconcile the main policy with careers, cookies, CCTV, call-recording, app, and research notices.

## Days 61 to 90

- Publish the layered design and task-oriented rights hub.
- Add contextual notices to collection points.
- Test the notice with representative users and accessibility checks.
- Publish the review date, owner role, version number, and material-change summary.

## Ongoing governance

Review annually and whenever a new entity, service, vendor, data category, purpose, transfer, retention rule, or automated decision process is introduced. Keep evidence of legal review, operational validation, accessibility testing, and publication approval.
