# Layered Notice Blueprint

## Layer 1: immediate summary

Display the controller for the current service, the data being requested, the immediate purpose, the principal legal basis, the essential recipient category, the key retention period, and a direct rights link. This layer should be readable without opening the full policy.

## Layer 2: audience and service modules

Provide separate modules for patients and examinees, digital-health users, website visitors, research participants, job applicants, employees, suppliers, CCTV visitors, and recorded-call participants. Each module should use the same processing table structure.

## Layer 3: full legal notice

Maintain the complete policy with definitions, detailed rights, transfer safeguards, security approach, complaint routes, DPO contact, and governance history.

## Standard processing row

Every processing purpose should identify:

1. service and controller;
2. purpose;
3. data and source;
4. Article 6 lawful basis;
5. Article 9 condition where special-category data is involved;
6. recipients and transfers;
7. retention trigger and period;
8. applicable rights;
9. whether the data is required and the consequence of not providing it;
10. whether automated decision-making is involved.

## Delivery pattern

Use just-in-time notices beside forms, recording announcements before calls, visible CCTV signage, research-specific information sheets, and a persistent privacy link in applications. All short notices should link to the relevant Layer 2 module and the controlled full notice.
