# GDPR Transparency Matrix

| Control area | Public evidence | Assessment | Recommended improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Controller identity | Group companies and contact details are listed | Broad coverage, but entity-specific responsibility can be difficult to determine | Add an entity and service selector or controller matrix |
| Purposes and lawful bases | Purposes and legal bases are described in narrative sections | Substantive coverage with limited traceability across processing elements | Use a purpose-level processing table |
| Special-category data | Health, genetic and biometric data are identified | Strong sector relevance; legal-basis wording should remain precise | Separate Article 6 grounds from Article 9 conditions |
| Data sources | Physical and digital collection channels are listed | Generally clear but spread across a long notice | Add source information to each processing-purpose row |
| Recipients | Medical partners, processors, insurers, authorities and group companies are described | Categories are broad and not consistently linked to purposes | Map recipient categories to each purpose |
| International transfers | Adequacy, safeguards and derogations are described | Mechanism is described generically | Explain how to request a copy of relevant safeguards |
| Retention | Several concrete periods and general criteria are provided | Mixed specificity reduces predictability | Publish a compact retention schedule by record category |
| Rights | Core rights, response timing and authority contacts are included | Strong coverage, but the path can be more task-oriented | Add a rights request page and request-status workflow |
| Automated decisions | The notice states that automated decision-making is not used | Clear current statement | Add a governance trigger requiring notice updates before deployment |
| Accessibility | English and Greek versions exist | Long-form structure increases cognitive load | Use layered notices, summaries and contextual prompts |
| Notice governance | A last-review date is displayed | Public date is July 2022 | Assign an owner, review cadence and change log |

The matrix records observable disclosure quality. It does not verify internal compliance.
